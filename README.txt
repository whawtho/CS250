How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

Interpreting user needs involves understanding what users want from a program and translating those needs into specific features and functionalities, with user stories helping by capturing requirements concisely. 

How do I approach developing programs? What agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

When approaching program development, incorporating agile processes like Scrum or Kanban promotes iterative delivery, frequent feedback, and collaboration.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development entails effective communication, collaboration, and a commitment to shared goals, fostering cohesion and excellence in project outcomes.
